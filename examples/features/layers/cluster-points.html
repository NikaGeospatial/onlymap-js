What this page shows

  • Clustering as a toggle: cluster="true" on the layer merges overlapping plants into count bubbles. Switch it off above to see all 21,000 points.
  • Aggregates: cluster-properties="sum:capacity_mw,mode:fuel" — each bubble is coloured by the fuel most of its plants use.
  • Sizes that survive zoom: the biggest bubble is always 36 px, the rest scale by share ($count / $count_max).
  • Picks: hover a bubble for its summary, click it to zoom to where it splits; click a single plant for its card.
  • Zoom bands: past z12 an icon layer over the same file takes over (visible-zoom-range).
