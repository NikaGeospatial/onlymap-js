Point layers too

Point layers can also be “populated in” — each rack drops onto the map one by one. 
<om-step
  duration="3s" populate
  layer="bikes" parallel></om-step>

Camera control

The camera can be programmatically controlled to zoom into specific features. 
<om-step
  action="zoom-to-feature"
  layer="regions"
  feature-id="north-beach"
  curve></om-step>

Camera angles

Viewing angles are flexible. 
<om-step
  action="fly-to"
  pitch="45"
  bearing="0"></om-step>
Camera smoothing is automated, just tell it where to go.
<om-step
  duration="2500ms"
  action="zoom-to-feature"
  feature-id="mission" curve></om-step>
